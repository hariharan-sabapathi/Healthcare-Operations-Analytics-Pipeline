Healthcare Operational Capacity & Throughput Pipeline
======================================================

                         CMS Hospital IQR Dataset
                                   |
                                   v
                    Local Landing Zone (data/raw/)
                                   |
                                   v
          PySpark Ingestion Pipeline (ingest_raw_cms_data.py)
                 - filter Condition == Emergency Department
                 - split into hospital reference + ED measure extracts
                 - validate row counts and key nulls
                                   |
                                   v
        Landing extracts: cms_hospital_reference.csv
                          cms_ed_throughput_extract.csv
                                   |
                                   v
        PySpark Standardization Pipeline (standardize_ed_data.py)
                 - standardize types (dates, numeric scores)
                 - clean text fields, pad ZIP codes, fill nulls
                 - deduplicate to one row per grain
                 - data-quality validation only (nulls, duplicate keys)
                 - NO dimensions, NO facts, NO surrogate keys
                                   |
                                   v
      Standardized extracts (data/curated/): hospital_standardized.csv
                                              ed_throughput_standardized.csv
                                   |
                                   v
                    Snowflake RAW schema (landing tables)
                                   |
                                   v
                          dbt Staging models
                stg_hospital, stg_ed_throughput (thin renames only)
                                   |
                                   v
                          dbt Mart models
        dim_hospital, dim_measure, dim_date, fct_ed_throughput
       (surrogate keys, measure categories, referential integrity tests
        are all built and enforced here, in dbt -- not in PySpark)
                                   |
                                   v
                mart_ed_control_tower (Snowflake STAR schema)
                                   |
                                   v
              Power BI Executive Control Tower Dashboard
        Median ED Arrival-to-Departure | Transfer Delay | LWBS Rate
             Psychiatric ED Delay | Hospital & State Rankings

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tool boundaries (each layer owned by exactly one tool):

  PySpark    -> ingestion + data-quality standardization only
  Snowflake  -> storage (RAW landing tables) + compute for dbt
  dbt        -> all dimensional modeling (star schema), business logic
                (rankings, categorization), and testing
  Power BI   -> presentation only, connects to mart_ed_control_tower alone

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Local vs. AWS: pipelines/config.py resolves data/raw and data/curated to
either local paths or s3a:// URIs based on the CMS_STORAGE_MODE
environment variable -- switching environments is a one-setting change,
not a code change. In Phase 5, Snowflake's internal named stage becomes
an external stage backed by a storage integration pointed at the S3
curated prefix. Nothing in dbt or Power BI changes.
